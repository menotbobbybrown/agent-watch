# Contributing to `agent-watch`

Thank you for your interest in **`agent-watch`**!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Pull Requests are disabled for this repository.**  
> `agent-watch` is self-maintained exclusively by **ModelNorth Engineering** (`@modelnorth`).  
> 
> If you have a feature suggestion or encounter an issue, please feel free to open a **GitHub Issue** for discussion.
